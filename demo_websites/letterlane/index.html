PRIVACY BY DEFAULT

Your words stay in this demo.

Letterlane has no mail server or network connection. The composer gives feedback without saving or transmitting your text.

No delivery

The send action is intentionally a local-only preview.

No account

There is no sign-in form and no user profile behind the interface.

COMMON QUESTIONS

Before you write.

Will a message be sent?

No. The composer is entirely offline and never sends email.

Is a draft stored?

No. The demo clears its form after showing the preview message.

Can I enter private details?

Please don't. Use fictional text only in this concept.
